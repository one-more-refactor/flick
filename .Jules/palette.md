## 2026-07-26 - Keyboard-accessible Design Mockups
**Learning:** Mockup interfaces (like docs/mockup.html) are highly effective tools for exploring interaction models, but they often lack keyboard-navigation parity with actual production sites, limiting accessibility testing at the prototype stage.
**Action:** When designing mockups, ensure list-like controls (like library/books listing) have proper role="button" or role="option", have tabindex="0", and implement standard keyboard list controls (Up/Down arrows, Enter, Space) to allow true-to-life visual and functional validation.
